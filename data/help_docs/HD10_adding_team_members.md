# Managing your team in the portal

## Adding a team member
Account admins can invite people under Settings > Team > Invite. The invite link is valid for seven days. There is no charge for extra portal users.

## Roles
Admins can manage billing, team members and security settings. Members can raise tickets, view reports and approve change requests up to their spending limit.

## Removing someone
Remove a person under Settings > Team. Their access ends straight away and their tickets stay on the account.

## Changing the account admin
The current admin can transfer the admin role under Settings > Team. If the admin has left your company, email support from a company address and we will verify the change with a director.
