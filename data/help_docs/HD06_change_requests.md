# Change requests

## What counts as a change request
Anything that adds new behaviour, a new screen, a new integration or a new report is a change request. Fixing something that does not work as agreed is a bug fix and is free.

## How to request a change
Use Support > New change request in the client portal. Describe the outcome you want and who will use it. Your account manager replies with a scope and a fixed price or an hours estimate within two business days.

## Retainer hours
Retainer clients can use their monthly hours for change requests. Unused hours roll over for one month.

## Turnaround
Small changes (under four hours) are usually live within a week. Larger changes are planned into the next sprint.
