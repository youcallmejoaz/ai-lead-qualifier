# Signing in to the client portal

## Resetting your password
Go to the client portal sign in page and choose "Forgot password". Enter the email address your account was created with. A reset link arrives within five minutes and stays valid for 60 minutes. If it does not arrive, check your spam folder and make sure the address matches the one on your account.

## Single sign on (SSO)
Clients on the Growth and Scale retainers can sign in with Google Workspace or Microsoft 365. Your account admin turns SSO on under Settings > Security. Once SSO is on, password sign in is turned off for everyone on your domain.

## Locked accounts
After five failed attempts an account locks for 15 minutes. Your account admin can unlock it straight away from Settings > Team.
