# How projects run

## Typical timeline
Discovery takes one week. Most automation projects are then built in two to four weeks, followed by one week of user testing and a launch.

## Kickoff
After the proposal is signed we hold a kickoff call within five business days. Please bring the people who do the work today and someone who can sign off decisions.

## Weekly updates
You get a short written update every Friday with what was done, what is next and anything we need from you.

## Delays
The most common cause of delay is waiting for access to systems. Sharing access in the first week keeps the project on time.
