# Pausing or cancelling a retainer

## Notice period
Retainers can be cancelled with 30 days notice. Send the request from the account admin's email address or through Billing > Cancel retainer in the client portal.

## Pausing instead of cancelling
You can pause a retainer for up to two months once per year. During a pause we keep your automations running and monitored, but no new work is done. A pause costs 20 percent of your usual monthly fee.

## What happens to your systems
Everything we built belongs to you. On cancellation we hand over code, credentials and documentation within 10 business days.
