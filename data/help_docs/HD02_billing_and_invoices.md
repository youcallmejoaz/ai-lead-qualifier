# Billing and invoices

## When you are billed
Retainers are billed monthly in advance on the 1st. Project work is billed 40 percent at kickoff, 40 percent at the user testing milestone and 20 percent at launch.

## Finding and downloading invoices
Every invoice is in the client portal under Billing > Invoices. Choose an invoice to download it as a PDF. Your account admin can add a billing contact who receives a copy by email.

## Changing payment details or the billing address
Account admins can update the card, bank details, purchase order number and billing address under Billing > Payment details. Changes apply from the next invoice. We cannot reissue a paid invoice with a new address, but we can add a credit note and a corrected invoice on request.

## Payment terms
Invoices are due within 14 days. We send a reminder at 7 days overdue.
