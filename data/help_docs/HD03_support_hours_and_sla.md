# Support hours and response times

## Support hours
Our support team works Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm UK time, except UK public holidays.

## Response times by priority
- Priority 1, a live automation or integration is down: first response within 1 business hour.
- Priority 2, something is broken but there is a workaround: within 4 business hours.
- Priority 3, questions and small changes: within 1 business day.

## Out of hours cover
Scale retainer clients have out of hours cover for Priority 1 issues. Call the emergency number shown in the client portal under Support.

## How to raise a ticket
Email support or use Support > New ticket in the client portal. Include what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and when it started.
