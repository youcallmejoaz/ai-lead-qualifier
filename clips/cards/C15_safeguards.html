What could go wrong
Three safeguards built in.
✋

Approval before send

Nothing is emailed or written to the CRM until a person clicks Approve. Auto approve is off by default.

⚠

Confidence flags

When the AI is unsure about a category, a field or a source, the item turns amber and waits for a person.

⛔

Injection guard

Instructions hidden inside an email are treated as data, never followed, and the email is flagged.

Also: personal data kept out of logs, API keys only in environment variables.
